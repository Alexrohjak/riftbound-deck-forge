# Deckbuilding transcripts — the primary source behind the doctrine

> **~30,000 words of transcribed video advice**, from four different people who do not agree
> with each other. This is where [`DECKBUILDING.md`](../DECKBUILDING.md)'s `community` tier
> comes from, and until now it existed nowhere durable.

## Why these are in the repo

[`DECKBUILDING.md`](../DECKBUILDING.md) opens by saying that advice which cannot name its
source is indistinguishable from assertion, and that *"every judgement EE makes carries a
**source** and a **confidence**"*. Its `community` tier was nonetheless attributed to nobody —
phrases like *"widely held"* and *"consensus across the guides"*, with no guide named.

That was not sloppiness at the time; the material was pasted into a working session, used to
write the doctrine, and then lived only inside a chat transcript. **A source you cannot open
is not a source.** These four files are that material, recovered and stored where the doctrine
can point at it.

| File | What it contributes |
|---|---|
| [`01-deckbuilding-is-cooking.md`](01-deckbuilding-is-cooking.md) | The method — start, sieve to 40, ratios, iterate. `DECKBUILDING.md` §8's "cooking, not baking" is verbatim from here |
| [`02-vendetta-upgrades.md`](02-vendetta-upgrades.md) | Card-level upgrade advice per Legend, and a tier read of the older Legends |
| [`03-the-all-unique-deck.md`](03-the-all-unique-deck.md) | A deliberate 39-unique build — the counter-case to the 3-of/2-of/1-of ratio doctrine |
| [`04-archetypes-and-construction.md`](04-archetypes-and-construction.md) | Archetypes, the triangle, and building one deck per archetype. The richest source for game-plan-first construction |

## ⚠️ What is NOT known about them

**The source URLs were not recorded with the text**, and they have deliberately **not been
guessed**.

A different working session searched for Riftbound gameplay videos and collected eight
candidate URLs; the one fetch attempted returned *"no transcript, captions, description or
chapter markers available"*. That search and this paste are not linked by any evidence, so
mapping these four transcripts onto those eight URLs would be invention — the exact failure
[`EE-BRIEFING.md`](../../EE-BRIEFING.md) §3 exists to prevent, committed in the one file whose
job is to make attribution possible.

Only **one speaker names himself**: Andre Zescu, who teaches about games and card games at a
university in Montreal, in `04`. The rest are anonymous, and one carries a sponsor read.

**If the URLs are recovered, add them here.** Until then the honest citation is *"transcribed
video advice, speaker unnamed"*, which is weaker than a link and much stronger than nothing.

## ⚠️ How EE must treat them

- **`community` confidence, never `fact`.** These are opinions from people who play well. They
  are not the rulebook and not measurements.
- **They disagree with each other, and that disagreement is the content.** `01` sieves the
  whole pool down; `04` starts from an archetype and fills toward it; `03` throws out the
  consistency doctrine both of them assume. `DECKBUILDING.md` §7 records this split and EE must
  not flatten it.
- **Auto-captioned, so lightly garbled.** Card names in particular are mangled — *"Ireelia"*,
  *"as inelia"*, *"Victor"* for Viktor, *"Master Ye"*. **Never quote a card name from a
  transcript as if it were the printed name**; resolve it against the card index first.
- **They predate the current set.** `04` closes by noting Spirit Forged is a month out and
  *"all the decks are going to change"*. Card-level claims in `02` are the most perishable
  thing here; the method-level claims in `01` and `04` are the durable part.
- **Never a decklist source.** A list read aloud in a video is not a list Forge has validated,
  and it was built from a collection that is not yours.
